Huejot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Huejot ("the app") is developed by Lancimator ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Huejot does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. We never receive your notes: the app has no servers, and it sends nothing anywhere. Your notes stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself, or your device's own backup or transfer includes them (see below).

What the app stores, and where

  • Notes, checklists, reminders and settings are stored on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Device backups and transfers: your device's own backup and transfer features can include the app's data, like that of other apps. On iOS this is iCloud Backup or a Finder/iTunes backup of your device; on Android it is a direct device-to-device transfer when you set up a new phone (Huejot opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup). These backups and transfers are controlled by you and by your operating system's provider (Apple or Google) under their privacy terms, not by us, and we cannot access them.
  • Backups you create with "Export backup" are saved to a location you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive). From then on they are governed by that service's privacy terms, not by us.
  • Home-screen widgets (where available) read your notes from storage shared between the app and its widget on the same device.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No sharing of data with third parties.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.

Permissions the app may ask for

Permission Why Required?
Notifications To deliver reminders you set on notes Optional
Face ID / Touch ID (iOS), fingerprint / face unlock (Android) (Pro) To lock the app; biometric data is handled entirely by your device's operating system and never reaches the app Optional
Exact alarms (Android) To deliver reminders on time Optional

Purchases

Purchases of Huejot Pro are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from the store that a purchase is valid, and keeps that confirmation on your device.

Children

Huejot is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete all of your data at any time by deleting notes in the app or uninstalling it. Copies in your own device backups are managed in your iCloud or Google account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
