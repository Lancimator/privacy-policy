Tunewren support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email lancimator@gmail.com. We read every message and usually answer within two working days.

When reporting a bug, please include your phone model, the operating system version and the app version (shown in the app under Settings → About).

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Install the app with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the paywall (Settings → Pro) and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store. On iPhone use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android see Google Play's refund page in your order history.

Do you collect my data?

No account, no ads, no analytics. What the app keeps stays on your device; see the privacy policy for details.
