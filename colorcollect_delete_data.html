Delete your ColorCollect data

Last updated: 2026-11-21

This page explains how to delete the data that the ColorCollect app (published by Lancimator, see the Impressum) holds about you, what is deleted, and what is kept.

For what the app collects in the first place, see our Privacy Policy.

ColorCollect has no sign-up. The app uses a random anonymous account id, created the first time it connects to our backend (when it first sends usage statistics you agreed to, or when you first use Group mode or share a link). We never ask for your name, email or phone number, so that id is the only way to find your data.

Option 1: Delete everything from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the ColorCollect app.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon in the top-right corner of the start screen.
  3. In the Privacy section, tap Delete account & all data.
  4. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

The app first asks our backend (Supabase, EU) to delete your anonymous account and everything stored under it. Only when the server confirms does it wipe this device. If the server can't be reached (for example, you are offline), nothing is removed, so you can try again later or use Option 2.

If this device used an earlier anonymous account it can no longer sign in to, the app shows that account id before deleting anything, so you can copy it and email it to us (Option 2).

Note: This is permanent and cannot be undone. Afterwards the app is back in its first-launch state: it asks again whether to send usage statistics, and creates a new anonymous account the next time it needs one.

Option 2: Email us

If the in-app deletion fails, the app no longer starts, or you have already uninstalled it, send a deletion request by email:

  • To: lancimator@gmail.com
  • Subject: ColorCollect data deletion request
  • Include: your account ID, shown in the app under Settings → Privacy → Account ID (with a copy button). If you don't have it any more, tell us the room codes you used, roughly when, and any montage links you shared (colorcollectapp.com/m/?id=…), so we can find the records.

We delete the server-side data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Deleting only some data

  • One shared link: open the montage and tap Stop sharing link. The link stops working at once and the image is deleted, typically within an hour.
  • Usage statistics: turn off Settings → Privacy → Send usage statistics. Events not sent yet are dropped and no more are sent. Events already sent are deleted after 180 days, or now with Option 1 or 2.
  • Photo locations: turn off Save photo locations and Share photo location with my group. Locations already saved stay on your device until you delete the photo or the montage.
  • A montage: delete it from My Montages. This only affects your device.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling ColorCollect removes everything stored on your device (montages, photos, settings, photo locations). It does not delete anything on our backend, and a reinstalled app can't sign back in to your old anonymous account. To delete the server copy, use Option 1 before uninstalling, or Option 2 afterwards.

An anonymous account that hasn't been used for 90 days and holds nothing on our server (no purchase record, room membership or live share link) is deleted automatically, together with everything stored under it.
