Earwren Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Earwren is an audiobook player made by Lancimator ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Earwren on iPhone, iPad or Android.

The short version

Earwren collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your books, listening positions, bookmarks and statistics are stored on your device, and we never receive them.

What stays on your device

  • Audiobook files you import. Earwren copies the files you choose into its private storage on your device so it can play them. The originals are not changed.
  • Your library and listening data: titles and other details read from the files, cover images, positions, speeds, chapters, bookmarks and notes, and listening statistics.
  • Settings, such as skip intervals, theme and sleep-timer preferences, and whether you have Earwren Pro.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by Earwren.

Device backups and transfers

Your device's own backup and transfer features may include Earwren's library data, which you control together with your operating-system provider: - iOS / iPadOS: iCloud Backup and computer (Finder / iTunes) backups can include Earwren's database and settings. - Android: Android backup to your Google account and phone-to-phone transfer can include Earwren's database and settings. Imported audio files are excluded from Android cloud backup to save space. You can turn device backups off in your system settings.

Purchases

Earwren Pro and tips are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment under their own privacy policies; we receive no payment details. Earwren checks on your device whether you own Earwren Pro.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android): used only for the playback notification with play/pause controls.
  • Motion sensors: used only while a sleep timer is about to end, to notice a shake that extends it. Motion data never leaves your device.
  • Files: Earwren only reads files and folders you choose, or files you open in or share to Earwren.

Links

Earwren can open web pages you tap, such as this policy, the terms of use and the store's purchase settings. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Earwren does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
