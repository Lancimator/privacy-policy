Tunewren Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Tunewren is a music player made by Lancimator ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Tunewren on iPhone, iPad or Android.

The short version

Tunewren collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your music, playlists and play history are stored on your device, and we never receive them.

What stays on your device

  • Music files you import. Tunewren copies the files you choose into its private storage on your device so it can play them. The originals are not changed.
  • Your library and listening data: titles, artists, albums and other tags read from the files, cover images, playlists, queues, favorites, play counts and when songs were last played.
  • Settings, such as theme, equalizer and ReplayGain preferences, sleep-timer options, and whether you have Tunewren Pro.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by Tunewren.

Wi-Fi transfer

When you open Wi-Fi transfer, Tunewren starts a small web page on your local network so a computer on the same Wi-Fi can send music to your device. It runs only while that screen is open, its address contains a random code, and it only accepts music and image files. Files go straight from your computer to your device; they don't pass through our servers or the internet. On iOS, the system asks for permission to use the local network the first time.

Device backups and transfers

Your device's own backup and transfer features may include Tunewren's library data, which you control together with your operating-system provider: - iOS / iPadOS: iCloud Backup and computer (Finder / iTunes) backups can include Tunewren's database, settings and imported music. - Android: Android backup to your Google account and phone-to-phone transfer can include Tunewren's database and settings. Imported music files are excluded from Android cloud backup to save space. You can turn device backups off in your system settings.

Purchases

Tunewren Pro and tips are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment under their own privacy policies; we receive no payment details. Tunewren checks on your device whether you own Tunewren Pro.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android): used only for the playback notification with its controls.
  • Local network / internet (Wi-Fi transfer): used only for the Wi-Fi transfer page described above. Tunewren makes no other network connections of its own.
  • Motion sensors: used only while a sleep timer is about to end, to notice a shake that extends it. Motion data never leaves your device.
  • Files: Tunewren only reads files and folders you choose, or files you open in or share to Tunewren.

Links

Tunewren can open web pages you tap, such as this policy, the terms of use and the store's purchase settings. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Tunewren does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
