Laterlark Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Laterlark ("the app") is made by Lancimator ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. Short version: your lists stay on your device, there is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only time anything leaves your device is when you search online (optional) or buy something in the store.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your lists and items (titles, creators, years, statuses, progress, ratings, notes, tags, links) and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We have no server and no copy of them.
  • Your device's own backups may include them. On Android, Google's backup and device-to-device transfer can copy app data; on iOS, iCloud and computer (Finder/iTunes) backups can. These backups are controlled by you and by Google or Apple under their terms, not by us. You can turn them off in your device settings.
  • Exports (JSON backup or CSV) are files you create and save where you choose; what happens to them afterwards is up to you.

Online search (optional): Wikidata

When you type in "Search Wikidata" while adding an item, the app sends a request to the Wikidata API (www.wikidata.org), run by the Wikimedia Foundation, to find matching books, films, shows, games, albums and podcasts. - What is sent: the text you typed, your language code, and a technical description of the app (Laterlark/<version>, with our contact address, as Wikimedia's rules require). Like any internet request, it also reveals your device's IP address to the Wikimedia Foundation. - What is not sent: no account or device identifiers, no advertising ID, and nothing from your lists. - Why: only to show you search results. We never receive, see or store your searches. The Wikimedia Foundation processes the request under its own privacy policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy - When: only when you search. The app shows a notice before your first online search, and you can choose "Keep it offline". - How to turn it off: Settings → Online search. With it off, the app makes no network requests at all, except purchases through the App Store or Google Play.

Details you pick from the results (title, creator, year, type and the Wikidata item id) are stored with the item on your device. Wikidata's data is released under CC0. No images are downloaded; covers are drawn by the app.

Sharing into the app

When you share a link or text from another app into Laterlark, it is read on your device to prefill a new item. The app does not open or fetch the shared page.

Purchases

Laterlark Pro and tips are bought through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles payment and tells the app which products your account owns; we receive no payment details, name or e-mail. Your Pro status is remembered on the device. See Google's or Apple's privacy policy for how they handle purchases.

What we don't do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

Permissions

Platform Permission Why
Android Internet Online search (Wikidata) and store purchases.

The app asks for no location, contacts, camera, microphone, notification or storage permissions. Export and import use the system file picker, which only gives the app the file you choose.

Home-screen widget (Android, Laterlark Pro)

The "Up next" widget shows titles from the list you choose. The app writes them to its own storage on your device for the widget to read; nothing leaves the device.

Children

Laterlark is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your data in the app: edit or delete items, export everything, or use Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device (device backups may keep a copy until they expire).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes, and significant changes are mentioned in the release notes.
